On the Age of Custody and Equal Shared Parenting — The 50 Strongest Sources

This collection presents fifty sources — scriptural, juristic, medical, psychological, judicial, statistical and legislative — selected as the strongest in a larger corpus of 398. Together they support a single thesis: custody by women ends at the age of discernment, seven for a boy and nine for a girl, after which the child passes to the father or to equal shared parenting; childhood itself ends at puberty, not at fifteen; and after parental separation the default arrangement should be equal shared parenting, because the child has a right to both parents and suffers from a father's absence.

Each card gives the source, its evidence level, the decisive passage — quoted verbatim in English where the source is English, given in the original with an English translation where it is in another European language, and translated where it is Arabic — and a short note on why it matters. The cards are ordered by strength of evidence.
